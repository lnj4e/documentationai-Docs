## Product Overview

Claim Clarity empowers teams to organize, manage, and collaborate on project documentation seamlessly. You create dedicated spaces for your projects, invite team members, and keep all docs centralized, searchable, and up-to-date. Our mission focuses on simplifying documentation workflows so you spend less time searching and more time building.

With intuitive tools for version control, real-time editing, and integrations, Claim Clarity scales from small teams to enterprise projects. Start by signing in at [claimclarity.ai](https://claimclarity.ai) and create your first doc space in minutes.

## Quick Start

Get up and running with Claim Clarity in just a few steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign In" icon="log-in">
    Visit [claimclarity.ai](https://claimclarity.ai) and enter your email and password.
    
    Forgot your password? Use the reset link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Space" icon="plus">
    Click "New Space" and name it for your project, like "Q4 Product Launch".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your First Doc" icon="file-text">
    Upload files or create markdown pages. Invite team members via email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize & Share" icon="settings">
    Set permissions and embed your space in tools like Notion or Slack.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  After setup, explore integrations in your dashboard for deeper workflows.
</Callout>
