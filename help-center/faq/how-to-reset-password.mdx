description: Quick steps to reset your Claim Clarity password.
---

## Password Reset

If you forget your password:

1. Go to `https://app.claimclarity.com/login`
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Check your inbox for the reset link
5. Follow the reset link and set a new password

## Tips

- Make sure to check your spam folder
- If you don't receive an email in 10 minutes, contact support
