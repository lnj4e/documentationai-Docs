---
title: Cannot Upload a Claim
description: Fix common issues when uploading a claim.
---

## Problem

- File upload fails or spins endlessly
- Error: "Upload failed"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check file format">
    Acceptable formats: PDF, JPG, PNG (max 10MB)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh and try again">
    Log out and back in, then retry the upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    This can resolve stuck uploads.
  </Step>
</Steps>
