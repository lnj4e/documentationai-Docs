## Step-by-Step Submission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login to your account">
    Go to `https://app.claimclarity.com` and enter your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Claims tab">
    Click "New Claim" at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out claim details">
    Enter required information and attach supporting documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Review your claim, then click "Submit". Confirmation appears if successful.
  </Step>
</Steps>
